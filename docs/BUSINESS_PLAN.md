Launch an automated email marketing SaaS business leveraging GoHighLevel Pro Plan infrastructure with programmatic client onboarding and Chuck's Sacred Seductive Marketing expertise.

- **Problem:** Small businesses need effective email marketing but lack expertise
- **Solution:** Pre-built, psychology-based email templates with automation
- **Market Size:** Millions of small businesses seeking email marketing solutions
- **Competitive Advantage:** Sacred Seductive Marketing methodology + instant automation
